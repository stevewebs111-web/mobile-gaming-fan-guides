Tank Stars Fan Guide

Tank Stars is artillery combat distilled to its purest form: two tanks, destructible terrain, and a wild arsenal of shells. This fan guide covers tank selection, weapon mastery, and the trajectory skills that turn random lobbing into surgical strikes.

Choosing your tank

Tanks in Tank Stars differ in armor, mobility, and their signature weapons. Heavy tanks soak up damage and dominate head-on duels, while lighter tanks reposition faster and dodge incoming fire. Your first priority should be finding the tank whose playstyle clicks with you - aggressive players love the high-damage brawlers, patient players prefer tanks with utility weapons. Upgrading your main tank's armor and engine pays off in every match, because surviving one extra hit often decides the duel.

Weapon types and when to use them

The arsenal splits into a few families. Standard shells are reliable damage. Cluster weapons spread damage across an area - perfect against opponents hiding behind ridges. Heavy single-shot weapons like the nuke deal massive damage but demand precise aim. Utility weapons - shields, teleports, double-damage powerups - win games when used at the right moment. The golden rule: match the weapon to the situation. Don't waste your one hit kill weapon on a full-health heavy tank across the map; save it for the finishing blow when a standard shell would leave them alive. Learning every weapon's blast radius and special effect is homework that pays off immediately.

Trajectory: the core skill

Every shot is a physics problem: angle plus power equals landing point, modified by wind. Start by learning the 45-degree baseline - maximum range for minimum power - then adjust. High-angle shots drop steeply onto dug-in opponents; flat trajectories skim under shields. Wind is the great equalizer: a strong headwind means adding significant power, and the best players check the wind indicator before every single shot. Practice by aiming at the same spot repeatedly until you can hit it blind - muscle memory beats calculation under pressure.

Terrain manipulation

The destructible terrain is a weapon. Blast the ground under an enemy to drop them into a crater where they can't angle shots at you. Conversely, avoid digging yourself into a hole - always leave an escape slope. Creating ridges between you and the enemy blocks direct fire while you set up the perfect arc. Some weapons specifically reshape terrain; use them early to build your fortress, not as an afterthought.

Duels, tournaments, and upgrades

Quick duels are the fastest way to practice and earn chests. Tournaments offer bigger rewards but demand consistency across multiple matches - bring your best tank and your full attention. Spend upgrade materials on your main tank first; a single maxed tank beats three half-upgraded ones. Chests are the main progression source, so play regularly to keep the unlock timers running. And study your losses: every missed shot teaches you something about wind, angle, or weapon choice.
