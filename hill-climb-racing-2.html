Hill Climb Racing 2 Fan Guide

Hill Climb Racing 2 takes the beloved physics driving of the original and adds real multiplayer racing, deeper vehicle customization, and a garage full of machines to master. This fan guide covers vehicle choice, upgrade priorities, and the racecraft that wins cups.

Picking your first vehicles

You start with the humble jeep, and it is more capable than it looks once upgraded. Early on, focus on one vehicle rather than spreading coins thinly across the garage. The jeep carries you through the opening cups, and your first big decision is what to buy next: the sports car for flat-out speed tracks, or the monster truck for rough terrain. Each vehicle has a distinct personality. The jeep is balanced, the scooter is nimble but fragile, the monster truck crushes bumps, and the formula car is a rocket on smooth asphalt. Learn one vehicle deeply before collecting them all.

Upgrade strategy: what matters most

Every vehicle has four upgrade paths: engine, tires, suspension, and fuel. Engine gives raw speed, tires give grip, suspension keeps you stable over jumps, and fuel extends your range in adventure mode. The classic beginner mistake is maxing engine first and then flipping over on every hill. A balanced build wins more races: keep suspension within a couple of levels of your engine so the car stays planted. Tires are the unsung hero upgrade - better grip means faster cornering and cleaner landings, which translates directly into wins. Coins are always the bottleneck, which is why experienced racers plan around unlimited money farming in adventure mode, grinding the long downhill stretches where fuel cans and coin pickups are plentiful.

Adventure mode vs cups

Adventure mode is endless driving on procedurally generated hills - perfect for practicing throttle control and farming coins. Cups are the competitive heart: short head-to-head races against real players on fixed tracks. In cups, the start matters enormously. Time your throttle so you launch the instant the countdown ends, and learn each track's rhythm - where to go full throttle, where to feather it, where the big jump needs a nose-up landing. Ghost data from your best runs helps you spot the seconds you are leaving on the table.

Mastering the physics

Hill Climb Racing 2 runs on gloriously wobbly physics. The two pedals - gas and brake - also rotate your car in the air: gas tilts the nose up, brake tilts it down. This is the single most important skill in the game. Coming off a jump nose-down means a faceplant; a touch of gas in the air levels you out for a clean landing that preserves speed. On steep climbs, feather the throttle to keep the front wheels down - wheelies look fun until you loop the car. Downhill, use the brake to keep from going airborne at speed. Every track has a flow, and finding it is pure joy.

Events, teams, and daily play

Joining a team unlocks team events with big chest rewards, and donating to teammates speeds everyone's progress. Daily and weekly events offer unique tracks and generous payouts - these are the best coin-per-minute in the game once your vehicles are competitive. The public events with leaderboard prizes reward consistency over one lucky run. Check the shop's free chest timer regularly; those small coin and gem drops add up, and gems buy the cosmetic upgrades that make your garage yours.

Hill Climb Racing 2 rewards smooth inputs and smart upgrades more than raw aggression. Master the air rotation, balance your build, and race clean. For series background see Hill Climb Racing on Wikipedia and the official game on the Google Play Store.
