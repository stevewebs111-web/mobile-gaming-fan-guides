Robbery Bob Fan Guide

Robbery Bob is a stealth-comedy classic: you play Bob, a reformed burglar blackmailed into one last crime spree, sneaking through houses, casinos, and laboratories while guards, dogs, and cameras try to catch him. This fan guide covers stealth fundamentals, gadget use, and how to three-star every level.

Stealth basics: sight, sound, and shadows

Every enemy has a vision cone shown on the floor - if Bob enters it, the guard investigates, and a second look means you are caught. Move slowly near guards; running is loud and expands the noise radius shown around Bob. Walls block vision, so plan routes that keep furniture between you and patrols. Dogs hear better than they see, so creep past them at minimum speed. Cameras sweep fixed arcs - watch the pattern for a full cycle before crossing. The golden rule: patience beats speed on your first attempt at any level.

Loot priorities and star ratings

Each level hides loot beyond the main objective, and grabbing it all is what earns three stars. Coins are scattered in the open, but the big payouts sit in safes and locked rooms that require extra risk. Learn to read a level: the critical path gets you out alive, but the detours get you rich. Players chasing full completion often study unlimited coins walkthroughs to learn where the hidden stashes are before attempting a perfect run. Time also matters for stars, so once you know a level's layout, plan a route that chains loot pickups without backtracking.

Gadgets and outfits

Bob's gadget kit grows as you progress: the teleportation mine lets you blink past guards, the wind-up decoy distracts patrols, and the invisibility-style gadgets buy precious seconds in camera rooms. Gadgets are limited per level, so save them for the rooms you cannot solve cleanly. Outfits are more than cosmetic - some reduce noise, others increase speed or starting gadgets. Match the outfit to the level: stealth suits for guard-heavy mansions, speed outfits for timed challenges.

Enemy types and how to beat them

Standard guards patrol fixed routes - memorize the route, then move in the gaps. Butlers move slower but look around more. Dogs charge when they hear you, so never run near them. Scientists in the lab levels trigger alarms that lock doors, turning a stealth run into a frantic sprint. The trickiest levels combine all of these: lure a guard away with a decoy, slip past the dog while it investigates, and time the camera sweep. When an alarm does go off, don't panic - sprint for the exit using the route you already scouted.

Three-star strategies

To three-star consistently, do a scouting run first: walk the level slowly, note guard patterns, camera arcs, and loot locations, then restart with a plan. Prioritize the farthest loot first so your escape route is downhill. Use gadgets to skip the hardest single room rather than the whole level. And remember that some levels have alternate paths - vents, back doors, and windows that bypass the obvious guarded hallway. The game's charm is in experimentation, so try the silly route at least once.
