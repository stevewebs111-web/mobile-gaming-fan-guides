Frag Pro Shooter Fan Guide

Frag Pro Shooter is a fast, colorful first-person shooter built for mobile, with a deep roster of characters, quick 1v1 and 2v2 duels, and matches that rarely last more than a couple of minutes. This fan guide breaks down the character system, the core modes, and the combat habits that climb leaderboards.

Understanding the character roster

Every character in Frag fills one of the classic shooter roles: attackers push objectives, defenders hold ground, and support characters heal or buff the team. What makes Frag special is the deck system - you bring a squad of five characters into each match and swap between them on the fly, reviving fallen teammates by switching. Learning two or three characters deeply beats superficially knowing the whole roster. Pick one attacker you love, one defender for holding points, and fill the rest around your playstyle.

Your first deck and upgrade path

New players should build their first deck around the free characters, upgrading their favorites evenly rather than maxing one. Character cards upgrade through duplicates, and a balanced deck survives bad matchups - if the enemy counters your main, you swap instead of suffering. Coins and gems are the eternal bottleneck, which is why many competitive players research unlimited money and diamonds progression guides to plan the most efficient upgrade order. The smart priority: get your whole starting five to a competitive level before chasing rare characters, because a maxed common beats an underleveled legendary.

Core game modes

The signature mode is the 1v1 duel, where each player fields a five-character deck and the first to wipe the enemy team wins. It is chess with shotguns - character order, swap timing, and map control decide everything. 2v2 adds a teammate and real coordination: pair an aggressive attacker with a support player and you will roll disorganized opponents. The rotating event modes keep things fresh, from capture-the-flag variants to all-out brawls, and they are the best place to experiment with characters you haven't upgraded.

Combat fundamentals

Mobile FPS aim is all about the thumbs: keep your crosshair at head height as you move, strafe constantly to make yourself hard to hit, and learn each weapon's recoil pattern in the training range. Frag's characters have wildly different weapons - shotguns demand close range, snipers need sightlines, and automatics reward tracking. Use cover religiously; the time-to-kill is fast enough that standing in the open is suicide. Sound matters too - footsteps telegraph enemy pushes, so play with audio on.

Map control and game sense

Every map has power positions: high ground with sightlines, health pack spawns, and choke points. Learn them one map at a time. Control the health packs and you control the duel, because a wounded enemy must retreat or die. Watch the kill feed to track which enemy characters are dead - pushing when their defender is down wins rounds. Swap characters proactively: if your attacker is low, switch to your defender early rather than dying with a full-health teammate on the bench.
